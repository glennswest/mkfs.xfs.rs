## 2. Spec / reference tool: xfsprogs moved on from 6.15.0
Every golden was captured with 6.15.0. Newer releases with mkfs.xfs changes:
- 6.16.0: atomic-write max size option, log stripe unit autodetect for external logs.
- 6.17.0 (2025-10-20): rtgroup geometry copy-paste fix.
  [release](https://kernel.googlesource.com/pub/scm/fs/xfs/xfsprogs-dev/+/refs/heads/master%5E%21)
- 6.18.0 (2025-12): "mkfs: enable new features by default", "add 2025 LTS
  config file", zoned-fs fixes, deterministic time/seed for reproducible
  filesystems. [CHANGES](https://fossies.org/linux/xfsprogs/doc/CHANGES)
- 6.19.0 (2026-03-18): fixes to log sunit auto-configuration and to 512e/4kN
  disks in mkfs.xfs; protofile large-file corruption fix.
  [announce](https://lwn.net/Articles/1063506/)
- No v7.0 found.

The 6.18 default change and the 6.19 log-sunit fix touch what we hold as
"identical to mkfs.xfs" (see CLAUDE.md "log sector > 512 gives sb_logsunit").
Filed as an issue.
